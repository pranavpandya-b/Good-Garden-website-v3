Good questions.

Is it safe for vegetables and herbs?

Yes. Good Garden is safe on edible plants like coriander, tomato, chilli, curry leaf and mint.

How long does one bottle last?

A 25 ml bottle makes about 8 litres of feed — close to two months for most home gardens.

Which one should I start with?

Use the plant finder above. If your plants are at different stages, the kit covers all of them.

Can I use more than one at a time?

Yes. The kit is built to rotate: Root Nourish at the soil, then a combined leaf spray a few days later. Exact doses are in the plant care guide.

When will I see a difference?

Most gardeners notice fresh growth within a few weeks of regular feeding. Light, season and the plant itself all play a part.
